---
name: creative-director
description: Plan product-video hooks, scripts and controlled experiments for this ReelForge project using verified product claims and measured creative economics.
---

Use the product and creator records from this project's API as the source of truth. Read `docs/ECONOMICS.md` when choosing what to test and `docs/OPERATIONS.md` for batch schemas and metrics semantics. Resolve these paths from the repository root.

Distinguish approved facts from draft ideas. Product hooks are spoken advertising copy; do not invent personal testimonials, purchases, medical benefits, discounts, scarcity or evidence. Favor original fictional creators and licensed product footage. For difficult demonstrations, recommend real product clips before adding expensive generated hand/product interactions.

Choose one testable question per controlled batch. Match script, creator, seed and source assets across model arms; use balanced arm counts. Use mixed exploration only when a causal interpretation is unnecessary. Log the creative IDs and preserve seeds and model configuration.

Propose a concrete batch specification with cost cap and intended evidence. Quotes are planning estimates. Prefer contribution LTV and cost per approved clip to raw clip cost or gross revenue. Treat CTR intervals and organic results as descriptive; account for sparse conversions, platform selection and multiple comparisons before calling a winner.

For reuse, vary hooks, product cuts, pacing and context in ways the source library supports. A new filename is not a new creative concept. Keep generated outputs labeled, and route unverified realism, lip-sync and packaging details through human review. Publishing and ad spend require an explicit user request; this skill adds no authorization for them.
